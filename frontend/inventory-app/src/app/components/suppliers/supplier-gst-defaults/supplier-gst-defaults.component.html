A supplier default applies only where the purchased product has no GST rule of its own. Delivery and package charges use their own defaults and never inherit the product-line default. These are explicit settings: a supplier being registered for GST is not enough to classify its products. Saving them does not change the GST classification of any purchase that is already recorded.
